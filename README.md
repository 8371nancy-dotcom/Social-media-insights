# Social-media-insights
📊 Digital Lifestyle, Health & Social Media Behavior Analysis

🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to analysis how digital lifestyle habits and social media usage impact users' health, stress levels, sleep patterns, and engagement behavior. This analysis helps in understanding the connection between: Screen time and health indicators, Work patterns and sleep.

📁 Data Overview

The dataset contains user-level information divided into three main areas :Age, Gender, Country, Urban/Rural, Education Level, Income Level, Digital Usage Behavior, Daily Screen Time, Social Media Usage Hours Posts Created Per Week Likes, Comments, Messages per Week Notification Response Rate Subscription Status Health & Lifestyle Metrics Sleep Hours per Night Stress Score, BMI, Heart Rate, Blood Pressure (Systolic & Diastolic), Physical Activity Level ,Daily Steps Count

🔍 Key Insights

Users with higher daily screen time tend to have higher stress scores
People with longer work hours generally report less sleep
Lower physical activity is linked to higher BMI levels
Urban users show higher screen time and social media engagement than rural users
Younger users are more active in posting, messaging, and interacting online
Premium subscribers demonstrate higher engagement rates than free users

📈 Analytical Observations

A clear relationship is visible between digital usage intensity and health indicators such as stress and sleep. Engagement metrics (likes, comments, posts) increases with moderate screen time, proportionally Income and education levels influence both health patterns and digital behavior Notification response trends suggest that users with balanced usage habits are more responsive .

🏁 Conclusion

The analysis shows that digital lifestyle plays a significant role in shaping both health and social media behavior. Higher screen exposure is associated with increased stress and reduced sleep, while demographic factors influence engagement pattern This study highlights the importance of balanced digital habits and provides valuable insights for improving digital wellbeing, user engagement strategies, and health awareness.
